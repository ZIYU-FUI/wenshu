5. **See tool_use block** (= ReadFile) appear in response

**Pass criteria**: chat goes through v0.37 pipeline (= ConversationLoop + 7-connector), compression pill accurate, tool use works.

**Pass criteria**: panel visible, entries accurate, refresh on chat.
