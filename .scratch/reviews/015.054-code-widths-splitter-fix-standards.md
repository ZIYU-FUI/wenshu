## SUGGEST

### S1 — Commit body L17 has a unit-confusion between "-12 PT to absorb 3 splitters"

The commit body says:
```
1. toolsWRatio: 400.0/1920.0 -> 388.0/1920.0 (-12 PT to absorb 3 splitters)
```

But the math is actually:
- 3 splitters × 6 PT/splitter = **18 PT** of total splitter hit area
- 400 → 388 is a drop of **12 PT** in the column-sum
- The new sum 1902 + 18 = 1920 (= correct)

So "drop 12 PT to absorb 3 × 6 = 18 PT of splitter hit area" — the **-12 PT drop is the column-budget released**, and the **18 PT absorbed is the hit-area total**. The body conflates these by writing "(−12 PT to absorb 3 splitters)" without naming the splitter thickness. The fix would be to write it explicitly:

```
1. toolsWRatio: 400.0/1920.0 -> 388.0/1920.0 (drop 12 PT = 3 splitters × 4 PT
   absorber share; 18 PT total splitter hit area = 3 × 6 PT absorbed by the band)
```

Or, simpler and matching the new comment style at App.swift:78-80:

```
1. toolsWRatio: 400.0/1920.0 -> 388.0/1920.0 (-12 PT column to absorb 3 × 6 PT splitter hit areas)
```

This is **non-blocking** — the math is correct elsewhere in the body (L23 `1902 + 18 splitters = 1920`), and the in-code comment at App.swift:78-80 correctly states `to absorb 3 splitter hit areas (3 × 6 PT = 18 PT)`. The body line is just slightly imprecise. Recommend cleanup in next commit-message polish pass.
